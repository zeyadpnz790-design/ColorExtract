About ColorExtract

ColorExtract is a modern image color extraction tool built for designers, content creators, developers, and digital artists. It runs advanced color clustering algorithms entirely inside your browser, giving you instant, beautiful palettes with zero server latency.

Your Privacy Comes First

Your photos and graphics are processed locally on your device. ColorExtract does not upload, transmit, or store your images on any remote server. Your creativity stays yours.
